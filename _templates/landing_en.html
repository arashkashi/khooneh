A house in Qazvin, still unbuilt

How might people live well, close to one another?

A family, a two-hundred-square-metre plot in Qazvin, seven years, two designs by one architect, and still no house. The story is told here as it happened — the list the family wrote itself, the drawings that were made, the questions still open — for anyone who would like to continue the conversation: how, on an ordinary city plot, three generations, a guest and a caretaker might live near one another, share a garden and a roof, and stay good neighbours to the street.
