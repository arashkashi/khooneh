Chapter 2 · 5 November 2026

Values before options

I had produced three options, A, B and C. The review of them changed how the work is done.

Turning point. The owner's own stacking idea was set aside, and so were B and C. "My idea should be formed from conversation between you and I." B had also changed the 1399 plan while claiming to be based on it.
Decision. No more branching into options for now. First come questions, market evidence and values. The three options are parked in story/archive/options-parked/, off the page.

Reading 1399 again

Two corrections came from the owner, who knows this building better than the sheets show.

First, the empty box beside the first floor's loft isn't a void. It's the high ceiling of the room below, so the section now draws them as one room.

Second, the "parents" flat and the "second household" flat are one home, a big unit for a family with many children. The sheets support this: the north of the second floor has no kitchen and no door from the core, and a faint stair seems to link it to the third floor. So 1399 reads as three homes plus a caretaker, about 60, 135 and 170 m², close to the 60 / 110 / 190 the owner remembered.

Whether that big home still makes sense is open. The owner isn't sure, given how the market may change.

A shadow of the next phase

Stairs, columns, pipes, entrances and windows belong to the next phase, but the owner wanted the stacking to prepare for them: "a shadow of the next step here and now." Each option can now carry a coarse frame of these elements, drawn faintly over the section.

It already gives useful results. Of the ten wet rooms above ground in 1399, only three reach the main riser, which matches the distances worked out by hand in note 08. Sheets also disagree on one column line by 15 cm, which needs checking.

From values to specs

The owner asked for specs based on values, and for help finding those values. So the next step is an interview, as a page: nineteen values drawn from the 19-point brief and earlier notes, each turned into a measurable spec with a number to set, alongside questions and a stacking drawing to play with. The answers come back as text.

The interview also brought out a contradiction to settle: the 2024 notes put the basement last, while level −2 is now part of the idea.

Decision. This story is written as the work goes along, in this folder, so the reasoning can be used later.
