The story

33. A bed out of sight

11 November 2026

On 11 November you looked at the small duplex's upper floor and wrote: "I do not like the wall for the bedroom, it is such small space, but the problem is that when the wall is removed, and the main entrance door to the unit is open, then the bed is visible." You also pointed at the top left corner of the drawing, the big home's bath beside riser R3, as space the small duplex might take.

Privacy as a number first

Before drawing anything, the AI turned the worry into a rule, P-bed-sight. People stand on the landing in front of a home's open front door, at standing height. The rule asks how much of a bed, or of a sleeping mat in a bedroom, they can see. Walls, shut inner doors, the open door's own leaf and tall pieces such as wardrobes stop the eye. Beds, desks and sofas don't. The numbers are yours to tune: which doors count, the eye height, how much of a bed may show, whether inner doors are open or shut, and how far back people stand.

The sight lines are the design page's own "Sight from" layer, now in one shared module. The decision pages use the same layer, so every option of every decision can be looked at from the entrance or a guest's seat.

Today, with inner doors shut, no bed in the building is seen. With inner doors open, the big home's three bedrooms show from its landing doors. Without the small duplex's bedroom wall, 64% of its bed shows.

Six ways, measured

Each way was built in a scratch copy of the whole pipeline, then checked by every rule and every day-in-the-life scenario:

  • The bed moved east, out of sight. The bay there is too short for its foot.
  • Closing the upper door. The bed then has to come up the spiral, and the floor keeps only one way out.
  • Your corner, alone or with the open loft. It is good for drains, but it costs the big family its children's shower room and its laundry, and the small duplex's WC doesn't fit beside the riser.
  • The AI's recommendation: the bedroom's door moves up the hall instead of going away. It closes a small entry from the landing. Below it, the spiral landing, the bedroom and the alcove become one open room of about 20 m². Every rule and scenario stays as it is today.

Where it stands

Your choice is on the decision page wip-d-upper-open. The recommended option is built and tested on its own branch, and it merges as soon as you answer.
