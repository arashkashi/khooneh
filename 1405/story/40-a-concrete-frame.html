The story

40. A concrete frame

17 November 2026

You said on 17 November: "Please take into account the structure of the building, not metal bars but concrete. Make a safe estimation of the thickness and also position of them. I could later refine it with the help of an engineer."

So the building now has a structure in its data. It is reinforced concrete, sized on the safe side. It is a preliminary estimate for the engineer, not a structural design.

What the codes say about Qazvin

  • The hazard. Standard 2800 (4th edition) lists Qazvin among the cities of very high earthquake hazard, with a design acceleration of 0.35 g. A 5th edition exists; the engineer will say whether it applies.
  • The loads. Part 6 asks 2 kN/m² for the rooms of a home, 5 for a roof garden and 4 for the parking.
  • The sizes. Part 9 sets the smallest sizes: columns at least 30 cm, shear walls at least 15 cm, basement walls at least 20 cm.
  • What could not be confirmed. The usual Iranian rules of thumb (40×40 columns, a 25+5 cm tirchebloc floor) were not found in any citable source, so they are not used.

The system

On a plot 10 m wide with neighbours on both sides, the party walls are the only long walls without windows. They become concrete shear walls from the foundation to the top floor, now along the whole depth of the building. With the walls round the stair and the lift, they carry the earthquake. Beams and columns carry the floors. The floors are 0.20 m solid slabs on beams, and the foundation is a mat in two steps.

What changes in the plan now

Only what stays inside walls:

  • both party walls now run to the yard façade, which also carries the south-east corner (before, it hung 2.3 m out);
  • the stair house's east wall is concrete.

No room loses floor. The columns the plan already had are big enough: the most loaded carries about 2,400 kN at a third of its strength.

What is for you to decide

  • Shear walls across the plot. Across the plot there are too few, so the stair's street wall and the core walls should grow.
  • Beams and heights. The yard windows run into the yard beam, and the basement's shared rooms are 3 cm short under the beams.
  • Basement walls. They need 0.30 m.
  • The gap to the neighbours. It must grow to about 10 cm at the top.
  • Riser R2. It runs through a column.

Everything the engineer must confirm is listed in design/research/structure-1405.md. The "Structure" section of the design page and the sheets drawings/structure-*.svg show it level by level.
