55. The big home's door

19 November 2026

On 19 November you asked whether the grandparents' floor and the big home's living floor could be entered the way the small duplex is: from the landing's south side, straight onto the living room. "If we improve overall let us do this." Every way of doing it was built and measured, with the guest WC moved beside an existing drain each time.

Not on the grandparents' floor

On the grandparents' floor the idea lost. The wheelchair would have to turn on a landing too narrow to turn on, and the living room lost room for its furniture. That floor keeps the door it got the day before, opposite the lift.

On the big home's living floor, a small win

On the big home's living floor it won by a little. The door opens onto the living room. The sofa now gets up the stair and in, and the shopping reaches the fridge sooner: from the car to the kitchen through one door instead of three. The guest WC sits just inside, and the family's rooms stay off the way. The old entry's far end becomes a small nook of the family hall with a coat cupboard. The walnut lattice that hid the bedroom doors from the old front door is gone, because nothing private faces the new door. The home's floor area is the same; only its rooms are rearranged.

There is one cost. The couple's way from the front door to their bedroom now runs through the living room and the walk-through pantry, about 12 m instead of 2.5 m. The family's own stair from the floor below stays their second way in.

The door's width

The door sits 25 cm west of where the small duplex's door first stood, because that door, measured on the way, kept only 72 cm clear against the concrete wall. The small duplex's doors have since been moved the same 25 cm, and every front door in the building now keeps 87 cm clear.
