The story

49. AI decisions, kept open

18 November 2026

Looking at an older copy of the design page, you saw cards still marked "awaiting you" and asked for the other half of what you had asked that morning: make a suggestion for each, built into the design, clearly marked as the AI's decision, and keep every card open so you can review it and change it later.

A status of its own

The suggestions were already made and built that morning, but the cards had been closed as "decided", the same word your own answers carry. Now a card the AI decided for you has its own status, "AI decision, open for your review" («تصمیم هوش مصنوعی، باز برای بازبینی شما»). 65 cards carry it. The 19 cards you answered yourself stay "decided". The design stays built with the AI's choices: open means you can still change them, not that they are undone.

What you see

  • On the design page each of these cards says it is an AI decision, open for your review, shows what the AI chose and why, and keeps its Accept / Reject / Not sure buttons and its note box. Accept confirms it; Reject or Not sure keeps it open, and the note says which option you want.
  • The list "AI decisions, open for your review" on the design page and on the decisions index gives each one the line that confirms it, for example "Decision wip-sky: option B (confirmed)", and the lines that choose another option.
  • The one-at-a-time page lists them after any open questions, most important first.
  • When you confirm one or pick another option, it becomes "decided" with your name on it.

Checked against what you said

Every AI choice was read again against your own words: hinged doors only, curtains over small stores, fewer stores, open space, a stand-up shower beside every tub, furniture keeping the way clear, B's 0.9 m stair, the −1 floor kept free, storeys as high as a permit allows. Two choices did not fit and were changed:

  • How a walk ends. The AI had let a home's walk end at the living room's window. Your walk rule says every walk ends where it started, so that is undone. The dull last frame at the front door is fixed inside your rule: the figure turns round and looks back into the home.
  • The long wall on the −1 floor. The AI had chosen built-in shelves over a long bench with drawers. You kept that floor open and had its soffeh with drawers taken out, so the wall stays plain until the floor's use is known.

The film map got a clear suggestion too: the plan of the floor you are on, with the figure and what it sees, both floors side by side on a stair. It is already the walk builder's map; you see it first on the preview pictures, and films are made only on your go.

Changing any of it

Paste a card's line into the chat: "(confirmed)" to keep the AI's choice as yours, or another option to change it.
