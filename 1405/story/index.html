  70. 70. What are we missing?

The floors were too thin for what they hold: told the honest depth, ten wet rooms' drains no longer fall, and sinking just those rooms cures it. A room-by-room heat balance sized the boiler and the floor heating. Doubts that held, doubts that didn't, and six small steps waiting for a go. Nothing in the design changed.
