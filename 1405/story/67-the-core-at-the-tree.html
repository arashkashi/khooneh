The story

67. The core at the tree

21 November 2026

An experiment beside 1405. It changes nothing in the design. The owner asked: "research the local design optimum we are in, in high level of abstraction, and investigate if we could search around for some other solutions, I mean in terms of main walls entrances, etc. this is just an experiment". The result is a page of its own, the core at the tree, and it can be deleted without trace.

The optimum, from high up

Seen from far enough away, 1405 is nine decisions. The stair and lift stand in the street-east corner. The front doors open off a landing in the street half. The two shared floors split west and east, so the caretaker and the small duplex both reach the sunny yard. The court is sunk in the yard with the birch, and three riser stacks and two party walls hold the rest. Every round since the first refined rooms, doors, furniture, kitchens and windows inside those nine. None of the rounds could move them: the core's walls run from the lowest floor to the roof, the room search never moves a wall across a column, and the stair box and the stretcher lift were parked in the triage because both need the core's wall moved.

Searching wider

So the search drew the whole building as blocks and changed only the big things: seventeen places and shapes for the core, nineteen places for an inner light court or none, and thirty-six ways to cut each shared floor. That makes 180,144 candidates, and 563 pass every hard test: the area bands, a front door on the landing for every home floor, four cars and the two-wheelers at ground, and a way through from the street to the yard. The first lesson came from the failures. Four cars rule out every core that does not stand on a party wall. Then the project's own sun-and-sky engine measured the best of them. The search's first place was 1405's own corner, made half a metre wider for the stair and the lift. The search had found its way back home.

The alternative

The best candidate with the core somewhere else puts the stair and a stretcher lift at the yard corner, beside the court and the birch. It is drawn on every floor. The whole street front becomes rooms, the big home gets three children's bedrooms, and the lift arrives at the court, while the court keeps its own stair as the second way out. It pays for this: the small duplex turns to face the street, with half an hour of winter sun in its living room instead of an hour and a half. The stair takes part of the sunny yard front. Two living rooms shrink, and the grandparents' kitchen loses its island.

The advice is to keep 1405. If the stair box and the stretcher lift matter most, the search's own first place buys them in the same corner, at a fraction of the cost. It is not free: the triage measured that half-metre at about two square metres of the big home, one each of the grandparents' and the caretaker's, two at the basement, and a narrower hall for the big home, and it waits on the engineer and the lift maker. To clean the experiment up, delete the folder experiments/radical-1405/, this chapter and its line in the story's index.
