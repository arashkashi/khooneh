Chapter 26 · 9 November 2026 · done, awaiting the owner

A kitchen that grows with the home, and a place to sit

The owner said the big home's kitchen felt small, and meant its proportion to the home. The AI measured all four homes. The big home and the first-floor home had the same 9.9 m² kitchen, 2.15 m wide, although the big home is 43% larger and has the bigger family. As a share of the home the kitchens ran backwards: the smallest home had the largest share. The big home's island stood half a metre into the living room. That is why a walk review found no sink in the model: the 3D model places a piece in the room that holds its middle, and the island's middle was in the living room.

Decided (owner, 9 November).
  • The kitchen line moves 1.0 m east. The kitchen is 3.15 × 4.6 m, 14.5 m², 9.2% of the home; the living room is 31.5 m². The island grows from 2.0 to 2.4 m and now stands wholly in the kitchen, with its sink and dishwasher. It keeps 1.0 m clear on every side and 1.2 m to the hob wall. The table for eight keeps 0.9 m all round, 1.3 m from the new line.
  • A soffeh at the living room's east end, under the high bay: a sitting platform 0.40 m high and 1.2 × 4.5 m, with seven drawers below, a toshak and poshti on top, rounded edges and a contrasting nosing. Its edge seats about seven without fetching chairs. It is off the balcony route.
  • The television keeps the one wall at right angles to the yard glass. It hangs on the east wall over the platform's back, about 1.9 m from the sofa. The armchair leaves.
  • Rules now hold the proportions on every build: the kitchen grows with the household and the home (K-size), is wide enough for two cooks in a family of four (K-width), inside walls stay under 0.30 m per m² of each floor (P-walls), a bath or WC on every bedroom floor and a guest WC by a duplex's front door (H-bath), halls take at most 22% (C-circ), no 5-60 cm slot beside a bed (F7-slot), and nothing tall in front of a window (F8-window).

The small window at the platform's end has a fixed lower pane to 1.0 m above the floor, only 0.60 m above the platform, and opens above that. A child standing on the platform could reach it. It gets a lockable restrictor, or a fixed pane raised to 1.5 m.

What it asks of the owner.
  • Is the big home for four people or five? The project counts four sleepers; the personas include a toddler.
  • In three couple bedrooms (the first floor's, the caretaker's and the big home's) the 0.6 m bedding store stands at the corner of a street window. No arrangement of the room keeps it clear of the window without blocking a way or a bed side. Keep it there, move the bedding into the wardrobe's top, or narrow the window?
