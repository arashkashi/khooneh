Chapter 14 · 7 November 2026

The form stays open

The form has done its first job. The owner filled it in, the answers are kept as a file in design/answers/, and they shaped the first AI version. It could have been put away at that point. The owner wants it to stay available instead, so that new people can answer it in the future and save their own .txt file.

The form.
The owner's local copy, on this computer.

It works the way it did when it first left home. Anyone with the link fills it in, and the finished answers reach us only when they save the .txt and send it back. The server keeps no answers, though progress saved in cookies travels to it whenever the page is reopened, over this link's plain HTTP.

Keeping it open showed a small problem. On a shared browser, the next person would find the previous person's answers and counter still there. So on 7 November the form gained a “New form” button (in Persian, «فرم تازه»). It sets the counter back to zero and empties the form.

Decision. Nothing is erased without a second press. If the answers aren't saved, the button offers “Save as .txt first”. If they're unfinished, it says they can't be saved yet.

The wording changed too. At the owner's request, the form now says “AI” («هوش مصنوعی») and never names the product behind it. The build stops if the name gets back in. This chapter follows the same rule and says “the AI”.

There are still two versions. The shared link has 42 questions and 17 values, for people outside the family. The local copy adds the owner-only questions, which makes 58 questions and 17 values, and it also holds the AI's design answers.
