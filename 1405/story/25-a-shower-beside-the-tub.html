Chapter 25 · evening of 8 November 2026 · done, awaiting the owner

A shower beside the tub

On 7 November the owner chose a tub with the shower over it, so that the bath would also fit a basin and a washing machine. A day later they changed their mind, for a plain reason: "people do not want to bath all the time".

A shower over a tub has a hidden cost. To wash standing up, you step over a rim 0.55 m high onto a wet, curved floor. That is the most common way people fall in a bathroom, and the people most at risk are the old and the very young.

Decided. Every bath with a tub also gets a separate stand-up shower, at least 0.9 × 0.9 m. A new rule, W10, checks it on every build. In the step-free home on the first floor, every shower must also be level, with no tray to step over.
  • The caretaker's bath (second floor, 6.9 m²) keeps everything: the tub along its west side, a 0.9 m shower in the far corner, the WC, the basin and the washing machine, with every wet-room rule still passing.
  • The big home's top-floor bath was too narrow for a second wet place. It got the 0.2 m that the narrower family stair gave up, and grew from 5.0 to 5.6 m². That was still not enough while its door stood in the middle of the wall, because the door's clear space blocked both corners. So the door moved 0.55 m east. The tub now runs along the stair wall, the shower stands in the far corner, and the WC and basin face them.
  • The first-floor home for older parents already has a level walk-in shower with a seat instead of a tub. Its name still said "tub", and that is now corrected. The AI measured the other way too: a tub and a separate level shower would fit, keeping the 1.5 m turning circle, if the washing machine moved to the store next door. That choice is the owner's.

One price is paid in the caretaker's bath. Its drain riser is far away, so the new shower and the floor drain each run a little over 3 m of pipe, the length an unvented branch allows. The tub was already over that limit. The plumber will need a vent or a slightly deeper floor there.

What it asks of the owner.
  • For the first-floor home, choose one: keep the level walk-in shower only, or have a tub too, with the washing machine moved to the store.
  • The owner's corner tub sketch no longer fits the big home's top-floor bath once a separate shower is required. Choose one: a straight tub with a shower, or the corner tub without one.
