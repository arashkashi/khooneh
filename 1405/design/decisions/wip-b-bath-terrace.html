Since nothing is built, the bath goes on the permit drawings from the start; enclosing it after the permit would be extra floor area for the Article 100 commission (a fine of half to three times the value per m², or demolition beyond the allowed density). What decides it is Qazvin's density allowance for this plot, which the project has not found: to check with the permit office. The F4 plate becomes 137.0 m², the same as F3 and the body below; ground cover and height do not change, and no setback or height rule made this terrace: it was a design choice from iteration 2. The street face changes: the 1.7 m wall goes up to the roof with a window in line with those below, and the notch at the roof line closes. Drainage: the nearest riser is R3, 3.6 m away.
