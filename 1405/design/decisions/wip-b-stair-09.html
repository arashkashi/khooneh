It still has 18 steps of 17.8 cm for the 3.2 m between the floors, needs 2.98 m of its 3.3 m length, and has 4 m of headroom. On the top floor the stair's opening stays open, with rails, to the kitchen and the living room. One cost: the bathroom's wall now comes 5 cm closer to the corner of the kitchen island, which keeps 1.0 m clear everywhere except over its last 5 cm (0.95 m); moving the island would narrow the aisle to the worktop, and that kitchen waits for your decision, so it stays flagged.
