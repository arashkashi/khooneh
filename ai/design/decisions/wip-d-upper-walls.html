What is being decidedچه چیزی تصمیم گرفته می‌شود

D's upper floor: fewer walls; the spiral stands in an open landing; the closet becomes a curtained wardrobe alcove. You asked whether there were too many walls on the small duplex's third floor (about 25 m² in five rooms). Now: the spiral stair and the upper hall are one open landing of about 8.6 m², with a rail round the stair's opening; the wall and door between the landing and the bedroom stay, and the shower room keeps its walls and door. The wardrobe room loses its wall and door and becomes a 3.1 m² alcove open to the bedroom, with rail and shelves behind a curtain, so the bedroom and its wardrobe read as one room of 15.4 m². The separate bedding cupboard in the bedroom goes; the alcove holds the clothes and the bedding. One change you should know: the bedroom is 2.75 m deep and has a column by the yard wall, and with a 1.6 m bed there was no free way to one side of it (0.52 m past its foot), so the bed is now a 1.4 m double with its head on the west wall and 0.6 m free on both sides.
