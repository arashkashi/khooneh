Chapter 5 · 6 November 2026, after midnight

The midnight review

A review had been scheduled for after the overnight run. Every test passed, so it recomputed the numbers from the room data and walked the drawings by hand instead.

Turning point. The redraw had passed its route tests, yet nobody could walk from the street to the sunken court. The walkway reached the yard, and the court stair stood on its far side, across ledges 0.2 and 0.44 m wide. The data treats the yard as one room, so the test saw a connection that wasn't there.
Decision. The court stair moves to the west side, at the end of the walkway, and the court shifts 0.5 m east. The shared room and the caretaker's living room also gain doors onto the court, because the only way in had been that stair, which shut out anyone who can't climb. New tests hold both in place.

Numbers that outlived their drawing

The flats of 103 and 113 m² were the critic's figures for the first draft, and they had been copied into the form after the redraw. The current rooms give about 106 and 107 m². The AI's stack also showed the plot's default of five parking spaces, when only four are drawn.

What the total was hiding

The “69 m² shared” figure sounded generous. The shared room itself is about 17 m², and the rest is plant, stores, halls, changing and sauna. The basement was a founding idea of 1399, and this design carries it less well than its total suggests. The form now says so, and it is a real question for the next iteration.
